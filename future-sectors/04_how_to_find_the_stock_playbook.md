# 4) Playbook: pick the sector → pick the stock → check if the play is already priced in

## Step 1 – Choose the sector (1–2 hours per sector)
Score each sector 0–2 on every line; pick sectors scoring ≥ 8/12.
1. **Size & growth:** credible 12%+ CAGR from a primary or multi-source number (file 2)
2. **Policy/structural push:** budget line, scheme, target, import ban (PIB, Budget, ministry)
3. **Demand visibility:** orders, tenders, capacity announcements already happening
4. **Profit pool:** are margins/ROCE decent *across* the chain, or only for a few players?
5. **Barriers:** licences, certifications, qualification cycles, capex, IP
6. **Timing:** are we early (policy done, revenue starting) or late (everyone talking)?

## Step 2 – Map the value chain
Write the chain on one page (e.g. data centre: land → power → transformers/cables → cooling → servers → software). Ask: *which link has the fewest competitors and the most pricing power?* That link, not the headline name, is usually the better stock.

## Step 3 – Build the stock universe (screener.in / NSE / BSE)
- Search by sector, industry and keywords in annual reports/concall transcripts ("data centre", "defence orders", "ECMS").
- Filters for early-stage candidates: market cap ₹500–20,000 cr · sales growth last 2–4 quarters > 25% YoY · order book / sales ≥ 1.5× · debt/equity < 1 · promoter holding > 40% and pledge < 5%.
- Drop anything you can't explain in two sentences.

## Step 4 – Is the play "already updated" (priced in)? – five tests
| Test | Where to look | Already priced in if… | Still early if… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price move | 1-, 3-yr chart | up 3×+ in 12–24 months with no earnings catch-up | flat/modest while orders rise |
| Valuation vs growth | P/E, EV/EBITDA, PEG vs peers & own 5-yr range | P/E far above growth rate and peers | P/E in line/below peers despite faster growth |
| Revenue proof | Quarterly results | story in news, revenue still flat | revenue/order inflow inflecting for 2–3 quarters |
| Coverage | Analyst count, MF holding, brokerage initiations | many initiations, MF/FII holding spiking | few/no analysts, low institutional holding, rising slowly |
| Narrative crowding | Social media, TV, "theme ETF" launches | every blog lists it | little talk |

Rule of thumb: buy when **fundamentals have started to move but price/coverage haven't**. If price has run but earnings are about to follow (and valuation is sane on forward numbers), it's "partially priced" – size smaller.

## Step 5 – Read the primary documents (the part most people skip)
1. Last 4 concall transcripts: does management hit its own guidance? Do they give numbers or slogans?
2. Annual report: order book by customer, capex plan, segment margins, related-party notes, auditor remarks.
3. Cash conversion: operating cash flow ÷ EBITDA > 70% over 3 years.
4. Capacity vs utilisation: new plant timelines and who the committed customers are.

## Step 6 – Management & risk check (file 6 scorecard)
Reject on any red flag; require ≥ 70/100 to proceed.

## Step 7 – Decide size and exits
- Starter 1/3 position; add on proof (next 2 quarters meet thesis).
- Write the thesis and 3 kill criteria in advance (e.g. order inflow stops, margin < X, promoter pledges shares, receivable days balloon).
- Max single small-cap position 3–5% of portfolio; theme cap 20%.
- Review every quarter result.
