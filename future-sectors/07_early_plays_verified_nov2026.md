# 7) Early plays – checked against news and results (18 Nov 2026)

Method: for each shortlist name I searched recent results/news to test "has the story started, and how far?". **I did NOT check share prices or valuations**, so "priced in" is unknown – do file 4, step 4 before acting. Not investment advice.

## Ranking by earliness

### Tier 1 – story just started, revenue not yet showing (earliest)
| Name | Evidence found | What's unproven | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kaynes Technology – OSAT/semicon arm** | Order book ₹10,563 cr at Q2 FY27 (₹8,904 cr in Q1). ₹3,307 cr Sanand plant inaugurated 31 Mar 2026; first commercial multi-chip module shipped to a US firm. Sources conflict on when volume production starts (Q3 vs Q4 FY27). One unverified claim: JPMorgan sees ₹250–300 cr OSAT revenue in FY27 | Volume ramp, yields, customer count; Q1 FY27 profit missed estimates (₹56.4 cr, down YoY per one source) | Execution and capital-burn risk. The stock is already widely followed |
| **Nuclear supply chain (MTAR, L&T, BHEL, Walchandnagar, Power Mech)** | Policy is done: SHANTI Act passed Dec 2025 (private entities can be licensed; 49% cap on private/foreign stakes); NPCIL issued a Bharat Small Reactor (220 MW) fleet RFP; ₹20,000 cr Nuclear Energy Mission, 5 indigenous SMRs by 2033 | **I found no supplier orders or contract awards.** Not checked: MTAR's own nuclear order status | Years from revenue; this is an option, not an earnings story. Do not size it as a core holding |

### Not verified in this pass
Apollo Micro Systems, Data Patterns, Astra Microwave, Zen Technologies, Anant Raj, E2E Networks, Netweb, Shilchar, CG Power (OSAT), MosChip, Exide/Amara Raja (lithium). Their stage tags in file 5 remain hypotheses.

## What this means
1. Genuinely early (policy done, little revenue): **nuclear suppliers**; **OSAT** is the earlier of two semiconductor stories because volumes are just starting.
2. Best risk/reward test for "early + proven": **transformer/grid makers** – orders are real, visibility is 18–24 months, but check margins and price first.
3. Don't call Kernex early. The market has seen it.
