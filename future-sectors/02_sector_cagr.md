## How to read this
- 10–25% sector CAGR is excellent but **already widely known**; the stock-picking edge is in *who captures it*, not in the sector number.
- Sector growth ≠ stock growth: capacity-heavy sectors often grow at 20% while returns on capital stay poor. See file 6.
- Gaps to fill with primary reports: nuclear (private participation rules), transmission capex (National Electricity Plan), railways capex (Union Budget), hydrogen (SIGHT scheme).
