# 3) Why and which stocks benefit – by sector

**Candidates to research, not recommendations.** Lists come from sector knowledge + search results; I did not check current valuation, results or governance. Run file 4 and file 6 on every name.

## A. Defence & aerospace
**Why:** ₹3 lakh cr production target by 2029; 92% of FY25 contracts went to domestic firms; private sector ~21% of production; export push; import-ban lists force local sourcing.
**Who benefits (value chain):**
- Platforms/yards: HAL, Mazagon Dock, GRSE, Cochin Shipyard
- Electronics/sensors (highest barrier – long qualification): BEL, Data Patterns, Astra Microwave, Apollo Micro Systems
- Simulators/drones/munitions: Zen Technologies, Solar Industries, Paras Defence
- Components/forgings: Bharat Forge (defence arm), MTAR (also nuclear/space)
**Watch-outs:** lumpy orders, government payment delays, valuations already stretched after 2023–25 rally.

## B. Electronics manufacturing (EMS) & components / semiconductors
**Why:** ECMS scheme (₹40,000 cr outlay, ₹1.15 lakh cr commitments), China+1, mobile/IT/auto/industrial electronics localisation, OSAT/ATMP plants starting.
**Who:** Dixon, Kaynes, Syrma SGS, Amber, Avalon, Centum, PG Electroplast (EMS); Kaynes and CG Power (OSAT projects); MosChip (design services).
**Watch-outs:** thin margins in assembly, customer concentration (one OEM), working-capital stress, subsidy dependence.

## C. Data centres, power equipment & cooling (AI-linked)
**Why:** 1.7 GW (2025) heading to multiples by 2030; data-centre power demand drags transformers, switchgear, cables, cooling, backup power.
**Who:**
- Operators/land: Anant Raj, (Nxtra via Bharti Airtel), E2E Networks (GPU cloud), Netweb (servers)
- Grid/transformers: Hitachi Energy India, GE Vernova T&D India, Transformers & Rectifiers, Voltamp, Shilchar
- Cables/cooling: Polycab, KEI, Blue Star, Voltas
**Watch-outs:** AI-capex cycle can pause; capacity overbuild; some "data-centre" claims are marketing.

## D. Energy storage, renewables & grid
**Why:** CEA plan 34 GW / 136 GWh storage by 2030; 500 GW non-fossil target needs storage and transmission.
**Who:** Exide, Amara Raja (batteries), Waaree, Premier Energies (solar manufacturing), Suzlon, Inox Wind (wind), Techno Electric, Power Mech (grid/EPC).
**Watch-outs:** solar module oversupply and price crashes, Chinese pricing, ALMM/duty policy swings, battery chemistry shifts (lead-acid → lithium).

## E. Nuclear (policy-driven, early)
**Why:** Government push to scale nuclear and open to private participation (verify latest Atomic Energy Act changes and targets).
**Who (hypotheses):** L&T, BHEL, MTAR, Walchandnagar Industries, Power Mech.
**Watch-outs:** very long timelines, few orders yet, regulatory/liability risk.

## F. Global capability centres (GCCs) & office real estate
**Why:** ~$64B → ~$100–110B by 2030; 2,400+ centres; demand for Grade-A offices, talent, engineering services.
**Who:** Embassy REIT, Mindspace REIT, Knowledge Realty Trust, Brigade, Cyient/engineering-services names, staffing/ER&D players.
**Watch-outs:** AI could reduce headcount per centre; REITs are yield plays, not high-growth.

## G. Railways (modernisation & safety)
**Why:** record capex, Kavach anti-collision, wagons, Vande Bharat coaches.
**Who:** Titagarh, Jupiter Wagons, Texmaco, RVNL, Kernex Microsystems, HBL Engineering.
**Watch-outs:** tender-based, lumpy revenue, PSU competition; **no CAGR found in research.**
