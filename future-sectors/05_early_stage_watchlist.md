## How to promote a name from watchlist → buy list
1. Passes file 6 scorecard (≥ 70/100, no red flags).
2. Revenue/order proof in at least 2 consecutive quarters (or a signed order book that covers > 1.5× sales).
3. "Priced-in" verdict is *early* or *partial* (file 4, step 4).
4. Written kill criteria.
