## What the Q2 numbers say overall
- **Strongest evidence of positive impact:** Hitachi Energy India (backlog ₹33,335 cr), Polycab, E2E Networks, Netweb, Exide, Zen Technologies.
- **Revenue up but profit not following:** Dixon, Hitachi Energy (to a degree), Amara Raja. Watch margin before assuming earnings growth.
- **Sharp negative surprises:** Data Patterns revenue −57% (though order book is up), Mazagon Dock profit/revenue decline.
- **Not tested by results this session:** all [logic] rows. Do not treat them as validated.
