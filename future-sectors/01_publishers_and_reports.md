# 1) Who publishes "future sector" research

## Global think-tanks / consultancies (themes and long-horizon sizing)
| Publisher | Product to look for | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| McKinsey Global Institute | *The next big arenas of competition* (18 arenas: AI, cloud, semiconductors, robotics, batteries, nuclear fission, etc.; horizon 2040; secondary summaries cite $29–48T combined revenue by 2040 and ~29% CAGR since 2022 – verify in the original) | Which technology arenas are big |
| BCG, Bain, Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, Accenture | Annual tech/industry outlooks; Deloitte TMT Predictions 2026 (India compute & semiconductors) | Sector sizing, adoption timing |
| Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, UBS, JPMorgan | AI capex, data-centre power demand, India equity strategy notes (UBS: India semiconductor market ~15% CAGR to ~$108B by 2030) | Capex cycles, market-size claims |
| IEA, IRENA, BloombergNEF | Energy/storage/power-demand outlooks | Energy transition, grid, storage |
| WEF, OECD, World Bank | Macro and policy context | Policy tailwinds |

## India-specific publishers
| Publisher | What to read |
|---|---|
| Nasscom + Zinnov, FICCI + Anarock, EY | GCC (global capability centre) market: ~$99–110B by 2030, ~10% CAGR, 2,400+ centres |
| IESA, MeitY, ICEA, ELCINA | Semiconductors and electronics (IESA: semiconductor market ~13% CAGR to ~$103B by 2030) |
| Ministry of Defence / PIB, Rajnath Singh statements | Defence production ₹3 lakh cr and exports ₹50,000 cr targets by 2029 |
| CEA, MNRE, NITI Aayog, Ministry of Power | Storage (34 GW / 136 GWh by 2030 CEA plan), renewables, transmission, data-centre capacity |
| Savills India, CBRE, JLL, Anarock | Data centres and office real estate (Savills: 23% CAGR to 4 GW IT by 2030) |
| IBEF | Sector fact-sheets |
| Brokerage thematic reports (Kotak, Motilal Oswal, ICICI Sec, Nuvama, Jefferies, Avendus, Ambit) | Stock-level mapping of themes – usually free via broker or website |
| Market-research vendors (MarketsandMarkets, Mordor, IMARC, TechSci, Grand View) | Quick CAGR numbers – **lowest reliability, widely divergent** |

## Company-level primary sources (best signal of "is the story real")
Annual reports, investor presentations, earnings-call transcripts (BSE/NSE filings, company IR pages), order-book announcements (stock-exchange disclosures), DRHP/RHP for IPOs, SEBI filings (shareholding, pledge, insider trades).

## Reading tip
Prefer one primary source (government plan, company filing) over three vendor press releases. When a vendor CAGR is far above the others (e.g. the "99%" BESS CAGR seen in search), discard it.
