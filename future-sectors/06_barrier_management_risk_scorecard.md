# 6) High barrier + honest management + risk-taking scorecard

Score each line 0–5. Total 100. **Proceed only if ≥ 70 and no hard-stop flags.**

## A. Barrier to entry / moat (30)
| Item | What good looks like | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Regulatory/qualification barrier | Defence, nuclear, RDSO, utility vendor approvals taking 2–5 years | /5 |
| Technology / IP | Own designs, patents, hard-to-copy process | /5 |
| Capital intensity | Needs large capex that rivals can't easily fund | /5 |
| Customer stickiness | Multi-year contracts, high switching cost, repeat orders | /5 |
| Scale / cost advantage | Lowest cost or niche leader with > 20% share | /5 |
| Pricing power | Stable/expanding margins when costs rise | /5 |

## B. Management honesty & governance (40)
| Item | Check | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Guidance track record | Compare 4–8 past quarters of guidance vs actual | /5 |
| Concall candour | Do they disclose negatives, give numbers, answer hard questions? | /5 |
| Promoter holding & pledge | Holding stable/rising; pledge near zero | /5 |
| Related-party dealings | Small, arm's length, clearly disclosed | /5 |
| Auditor & accounts | Big/credible auditor, no frequent changes or qualifications | /5 |
| Cash conversion | CFO ÷ EBITDA > 70% over 3 yrs; receivables not ballooning | /5 |
| Capital allocation | Sensible acquisitions, dividends/buybacks when no growth use; no empire-building | /5 |
| Regulatory history | No SEBI/ED/tax orders on promoters or company | /5 |

## C. Risk-taking quality – bold but sane (20)
| Item | What good looks like | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Bets are staged | Capex phased against orders, not all-in on hope | /5 |
| Funding discipline | Debt/equity manageable; equity raised at fair valuations | /5 |
| Founder skin in game | Large holding, long tenure, reinvests profits | /5 |
| Learning from failures | Past misses acknowledged and corrected | /5 |

## D. Financial strength (10)
| Item | Check | Score |
|---|---|---|
| ROCE | > 15% or rising credibly | /5 |
| Balance sheet | Interest cover > 4×, low debt | /5 |

## Hard-stop red flags (reject regardless of score)
- Promoter pledge > 20% or rising
- Frequent auditor resignations / qualified opinion
- Revenue growing but receivables or inventory days rising sharply for 3+ quarters
- Aggressive guidance repeatedly missed
- Large unexplained related-party loans/transactions
- Pivot to a hot theme with no relevant track record ("we are now in AI/defence/hydrogen")
- Promoter selling heavily while promoting the story
- SEBI/ED action against promoters

## Honest-management quick tests (30 minutes)
1. Read the last 4 concall transcripts; highlight every promise with a date; check if it happened.
2. Search BSE/NSE announcements + news for "SEBI order", "show cause", "pledge", "auditor resign".
3. Check insider trades and bulk/block deals in the last year.
4. Compare cash flow statement to profit for 3 years.
5. Ask: would I trust this person to run my money for 10 years?

## Risk framework for the portfolio
- Small/micro-cap theme stocks: size 1–3% each; accept 50% drawdown risk.
- Never average down without new evidence the thesis is intact.
- Diversify across ≥ 3 themes; no theme > 20%.
- Keep a written exit rule (price drawdown from entry and thesis kill criteria).
