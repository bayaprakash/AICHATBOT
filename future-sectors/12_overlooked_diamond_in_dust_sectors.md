# 12) Overlooked "diamond in the dust" sectors (18 Nov 2026)

Sectors nobody posts about, where a few companies sometimes compound for a decade. **Be honest about the odds:** for every boring sector that produced a multibagger, many stayed boring or became value traps (cheap for a reason). The market-size numbers below are vendor estimates that disagree widely; the multibagger claim itself is **not supported by any source I found** and is my framework, not a forecast. Company names are research leads, not recommendations. I did not check prices, valuations or management.

## Why overlooked sectors can produce multibaggers
1. **Rules force demand.** Mandatory certification, recycling duties, or import substitution create buyers who cannot walk away.
2. **Small base, low coverage.** Few analysts, small market cap, so re-rating can be large once earnings are visible.
3. **Consolidation.** Fragmented unorganised markets move to a few compliant, scaled players.
4. **Long reinvestment runway.** Founders reinvest at 20%+ ROCE for years.

## How to separate a diamond from a value trap (screen)
| Test | Diamond | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | Sales and profit up 20%+ for 3+ years | Flat sales, "recovery next year" repeated |
| Return on capital | ROCE above 18–20% and stable | Falling ROCE, heavy capex without sales |
| Cash | Operating cash flow ≥ 70% of EBITDA | Profit without cash, rising receivables |
| Market position | Rising share; one of the top 2–3 compliant players | Price taker, commoditised |
| Coverage | Few analysts, low institutional ownership **rising** | No analysts because nobody cares |
| Management | Reinvests, owns shares, candid on calls (file 6) | Pledge, related-party loans, frequent fundraising |
| Rule/driver | Dated, enforced (EPR, BIS, PLI awards) | Announcement without enforcement |

## Recommended next deep dives
1. **Battery and e-waste recycling:** the clearest forced-demand story with listed names (Gravita, Pondy Oxides, Eco Recycling). Needs fundamentals, scrap-sourcing and governance checks.
2. **Medical devices:** large import gap but wildly different growth forecasts. Needs listed-name discovery first.
3. **Specialty chemicals:** treat as a contrarian turnaround (watch margins and Chinese pricing), not an early growth play.
4. TIC: low priority until listed pure plays are found.
